          MCProxVC Quick Guide
        

        
      


      

        
1. Voice Fade & Custom Range: The host can adjust the Max Hearing Range (up to 120 blocks) and Full Volume Radius at any time. Every player's Web Audio PannerNode and GainNode updates in real time.

        
2. Separate Spectator VC: When a player dies or enters /gamemode spectator (or clicks the Mode: Spectator button), they move to the Spectator VC channel. Alive players cannot hear Spectators, while Spectators can talk to each other globally across the map!

        
3. In-Game Chat Commands: When connected via /connect, players can also type !spec, !alive, or !range 45 directly in Minecraft Bedrock chat to switch voice channels or change the proximity radius!
